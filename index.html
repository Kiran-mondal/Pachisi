Rules of Pachisi & Chaupar

  • Players: Played between 2 to 4 players. Movement progresses anti-clockwise around the board arms.
  • Token Base (Yard): Tokens start in each player's respective Token Yard in the board corner.
  • Pasha (Stick Dice): Played with solid wooden stick dice. Doublets allow moving tokens and grant an extra turn!
  • Safe Zones (Pachisi): Marked squares with a golden star protect tokens from being captured.
  • Capturing: Landing directly on an opponent's token sends their token back to their Yard and grants you an Extra Turn.

The Royal Heritage of Chaupar & Pachisi

Pachisi and Chaupar are ancient Indian board games that were famously played by Maharajas, Emperors, and Royals. Dating back to as early as the 4th to 6th century, these games hold a significant place in Indian mythology and history.
