Rules of Pachisi

  • Players: Minimum 2, Maximum 4 players. The game is played in an anti-clockwise direction.
  • Tokens: Each player has 4 tokens. To start, tokens are placed on specific safe squares.
  • Pasha (Dice): Stick dice are used with values 1, 3, 4, and 6. A doublet allows moving a pair (Jodi).
  • Capturing: Landing on an opponent's token captures it. You must capture at least one opponent before entering your Home.
  • Safe Zones: The first 7 squares of each arm are safe zones (Belly).
  • Winning: The first player to bring all 4 tokens to the center 'HOME' wins.
