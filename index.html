Rules of Pachisi

  • Players: Played between 2 to 4 players.
  • Token Base: Tokens start in each player's Yard.
  • Pasha: Played with dice. Doublets grant an extra turn!

The Royal Heritage

Pachisi and Chaupar are ancient Indian board games that were famously played by Maharajas, Emperors, and Royals.
