Bharatiya Pasha Game Rules

Rolling the Pasha

Played with a pair of stick dice. A doublet allows moving a pair of counters together, or as individual counters.

Movement & Board

The game involves 4 sets of 4 counters. Minimum 2, Maximum 4 players. Pachisi is played in an anti-clockwise direction.

Safe Zones & Capturing

Squares 1 to 7 are called the 'Belly'. A counter cannot go into the bellies of other counters. A counter is captured when a player's counter lands on the other player's counter in the same square.

Path to Victory

The first player to get all his 4 counters home is the winner.

The Heritage of Chaupar

Pachisi and Chaupar are ancient Indian board games that were famously played by the Maharajas and Royals. They hold a significant place in Indian mythology, most notably in the grand epic of the Mahabharata. By playing this game, you are experiencing a piece of a 5,000-year-old tradition.

About the Developer
