Rules of Pachisi

  • Players: Minimum 2, Maximum 4 players[span_0](start_span)[span_0](end_span). Pachisi is played in an anti-clockwise direction[span_1](start_span)[span_1](end_span).
  • Tokens: The game involves 4 sets of 4 counters[span_2](start_span)[span_2](end_span).
  • Pasha (Dice): Played with a pair of stick dice[span_3](start_span)[span_3](end_span). A doublet allows moving a pair of counters together, or as individual counters[span_4](start_span)[span_4](end_span).
  • Safe Zones: Squares 1 to 7 are called the 'Belly[span_5](start_span)'[span_5](end_span). A counter cannot go into the bellies of other counters[span_6](start_span)[span_6](end_span).
  • Capturing: A counter is captured when a player's counter lands on the other player's counter in the same square[span_7](start_span)[span_7](end_span).
  • Winning: The first player to get all his 4 counters home is the winner[span_8](start_span)[span_8](end_span).
